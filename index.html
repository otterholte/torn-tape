Questions

Before you order

How does giving Torn Tape credit on my video work?

We'll give you one line of text with a link, like this:

Edit & visuals: Torn Tape → [your Torn Tape link]

Paste it into your YouTube description when the video goes live: in the first 10 lines, or next to your director and editor credits. On TikTok or Instagram, put it in the caption. Keep it there for as long as the video is up.

Credit is optional. It takes the price from $249 to $199, and it also means we can show your video and name you as an example on our website.

How long does it take?

3–5 business days for the first version.

What if I want changes?

You get 2 rounds of edits. Tell us the time and what to change, and we revise it.

Do you accept every video?

We review every submission before we start, and tell you within 1 business day, before you pay anything.

We don't take every project, sometimes due to the content requested or our current workload.

When do I pay?

Only after we accept your video. Send your request, and within 1 business day we'll email you a yes or no. A yes comes with a secure payment link, and work starts once you've paid.

Can I get a refund?

Once you've paid and we've started work, all sales are final. That's why you see preview stills first and get 2 rounds of edits after.

Will you change my colours?

Only if you ask. Keep yours as they are, or pick a style when you order.

What can I upload?

Your own music video, up to 6 minutes long. Upload an MP4 or MOV, or paste a Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer link. You must own or be cleared for the music and footage.
